# Football Hub expansion — 2 November 2026

## Evidence and limits
Audited the 46 existing public articles by title, introduction and section scope against ten proposed primary intents. Existing coverage includes first touch, scanning, beating defenders, home practice, weaker foot, winger, centre-back, shooting accuracy and choosing a local provider. New guides have narrower or separate questions; related guides are explicitly connected.

Semrush returned no_api_units. No keyword volumes, keyword difficulty or traffic forecasts were available. Web search confirmed an existing search-result ecosystem for passing drills, ball mastery, football turns, offside and private coaching. Search-result presence is a relevance/competition signal, not proof of high volume. Full-back, striker, shielding, throw-ins and corners are editorial opportunities supported by football relevance and gaps in this collection. No rankings or traffic are guaranteed.

## Implementation
Ten original guides, roughly 570–640 words of main text each. Each includes a direct introduction, specific sections, a practical activity, takeaways, primary-source further reading, three curated related articles and links to programmes and assessment registration. Existing metadata factory supplies unique title, description, canonical URL and sharing cards. Existing article discovery supplies sitemap, public search and hub listings. New articles record publication/modification dates explicitly. Existing articles remain present.
